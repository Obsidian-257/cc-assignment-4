Title: The Growth of Renewable Energy

Renewable energy is becoming increasingly important in the
global energy market. Solar and wind power have experienced
significant growth over the last decade.

Governments are introducing policies to reduce carbon emissions
and encourage investment in clean energy infrastructure.

However, challenges remain. Energy storage, grid infrastructure
and the initial investment costs continue to affect the speed
of adoption.

Experts expect renewable energy to represent a growing share
of global electricity production over the next decade.
